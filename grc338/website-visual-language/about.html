About Us.

Rogue Wave was born in 2015 in the heart of Huntington Beach — a place where the ocean isn’t just a view, it’s a way of life. Our story started with a small crew of surfers who wanted to share the stoke, chase the perfect wave, and protect the waters that inspire every ride. What began as a local passion project quickly grew into a movement built around community, creativity, and respect for the ocean.

At Rogue Wave, surf culture runs deep. We’re about early mornings, salt-streaked hair, and that unmistakable feeling of freedom that only comes from paddling out. Our gear and apparel are designed to embody that energy — functional enough for the lineup, but comfortable enough for everything that happens after. Every product carries the laid-back spirit of the coast and the drive to live boldly, no matter where the day takes you.

Good vibes aren’t just a tagline here; they’re our foundation. Whether it’s a beach cleanup, a local surf comp, or just a post-sesh hangout, Rogue Wave is all about keeping the surf community connected and positive. We believe that every wave should be shared, every session celebrated, and every surfer welcomed. That’s what keeps our tribe strong and our stoke alive.

Sustainability guides every choice we make. From eco-friendly fabrics to recycled packaging, we’re committed to respecting the ocean that fuels our lifestyle. Rogue Wave is constantly evolving to reduce our footprint and support initiatives that protect marine ecosystems. Because when you love the sea, you take care of it — and we’re here to make sure every wave you ride leaves the ocean better than we found it.
